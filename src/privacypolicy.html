GT 2 Logbook and your health data

GT 2 Logbook is a personal logbook for a Huawei Watch GT 2. It has no account, no server and no analytics.

What it reads from Health Connect

Read-only: steps, distance, active calories, heart rate, resting heart rate, heart rate variability, blood oxygen, sleep, exercise sessions, weight and VO2 max. It never writes to Health Connect. You choose which of these to allow, and can change it any time in Health Connect.

How it uses the data

Only to draw your charts and statistics on this phone. The data is stored in the app's private storage, is excluded from Android cloud backup and device transfer, and is deleted when you uninstall the app or choose Data → Delete.

When anything leaves the phone

  • When you press Ask, your question and the numbers Claude looks up go to Anthropic (api.anthropic.com) with your own API key.
  • If you add an intervals.icu key, the app downloads your intervals.icu activities and wellness; it sends nothing there.
  • When you download a map, the OpenStreetMap server sees the area requested, never your routes.
  • Exports and backups go only where you send them from the share sheet.
